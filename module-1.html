What's an Op Mode?

An op mode ("operational mode") is the computer program that defines your robot's behavior â€” follow a line, score a game piece, drive around.

The Robot Controller runs whichever op mode is selected on the Driver Station.

Two Habits That Save You Headaches

Label your devices

Tape and a marker on the Robot Controller vs. the Driver Station. It's easy to mix them up, and installing the wrong app on the wrong device causes confusing failures later.

Keep both devices charged

A low battery mid-match or mid-programming-session is a common â€” and completely avoidable â€” failure.
